## App Name

## Short Description

## Full Description
Motokah helps buyers and dealers find vehicles faster across Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and East Africa.

Browse used cars, new cars, bikes, boats and commercial vehicles by country, city, dealer, make, body type and price. Compare listings, check photos, review key details and contact sellers directly by call or WhatsApp.

Motokah is built for mobile-first vehicle shopping in Africa:

- Browse launch-quality listings from real dealers
- Search by country, city, make, model and vehicle type
- Compare cars before calling sellers
- View dealer pages with inventory and contact details
- Save vehicles to your wishlist
- Post your own vehicle for sale
- Find cars, bikes, boats and commercial vehicles in one place

Whether you are looking for a Toyota Harrier in Dar es Salaam, a Hilux in Tanzania, a dealer page in Nairobi, or a boat listing near the coast, Motokah gives you one cleaner place to start.
