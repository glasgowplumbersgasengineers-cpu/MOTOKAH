# Apple Physical-Device Recording Checklist

Use a physical iPhone on the latest available iOS. Record one continuous, legible video in portrait orientation. Do not expose private email, phone, notification, or password information.

## Before recording

- Install the new TestFlight build with build number 2.
- Confirm the review account can sign in.
- Confirm at least one vehicle listing has several working images.
- Confirm the review account has no sensitive personal information.
- Confirm account deletion works with a disposable test account.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb and hide notification previews.
- Use a stable Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection.

## Recording sequence

1. Start the iPhone screen recording before opening Motokah.
2. Launch Motokah from the Home Screen so the app icon and cold launch are visible.
3. Complete or show country/location selection, then reach the home feed.
4. Open Search, apply a country and one vehicle filter, and open a result.
5. Swipe through listing images and scroll through specifications and seller details.
6. Show Save, Compare, Share, Call, or WhatsApp actions without exposing a real conversation.
7. Open Report, choose a reason, submit it, and show the success confirmation.
8. Open another listing, choose Block seller, confirm the seller's inventory is removed from results.
9. Sign out if needed, then show registration and login. Password typing may be paused or obscured, but the completed flow must be visible.
10. Open Sell, add test photos, complete the required fields, and submit a test listing. Show its submitted or pending state.
11. Open Profile Settings, choose Delete my account, show the confirmation warning, and permanently delete the disposable account.
12. Return to the signed-out state and stop recording.

## Upload and notes

- Keep the video concise, ideally 3-5 minutes.
- Upload the recording directly in the App Review conversation if supported. Otherwise provide an HTTPS link that requires no login and does not expire during review.
- Paste `launch/apple-app-review-response-2026-10-07.md` into both Reply to App Review and App Review Information > Notes.
- Put working review credentials in the dedicated Sign-In Information fields.
- State explicitly: `The app has no paid content, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.`
- Do not resubmit until build 2 has processed and the recording link or attachment is present.
