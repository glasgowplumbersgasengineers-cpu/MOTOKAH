## 2. Purpose, audience, and value

Motokah is a public vehicle marketplace for East Africa. It helps adults discover cars, motorcycles, commercial vehicles, and boats offered by private sellers and vehicle dealers. Buyers can search by vehicle attributes and location, compare listings, save favourites, inspect listing photos and details, and contact the seller directly. Sellers can create an account and submit a vehicle listing.

The app addresses fragmented vehicle discovery across social media and individual dealer channels by presenting searchable listings in one consistent marketplace. Motokah does not sell vehicles itself, process vehicle payments, provide financing, or act as a vehicle dealer.

## 3. Access and main-feature instructions

Browsing does not require an account:

1. Launch Motokah and select a country or allow location access.
2. Browse the home feed or open Search.
3. Apply make, model, body type, condition, location, year, and price filters.
4. Open a listing to view its photos, vehicle details, seller information, and contact actions.

Account features:

1. Open More, then Sign in.
2. Register with an email address and password, or use the review credentials supplied in the App Review Information fields.
3. Signed-in users can save listings, post a vehicle, report content, block a seller, manage their profile, and delete their account from Profile Settings.

No sample file is required. The app includes visible marketplace inventory for review.

Review account credentials are supplied separately in App Store Connect's Sign-In Information fields. They should not be copied into the public App Store description.

## 4. External services

Motokah uses the following services to provide its core functionality:

- Supabase for account authentication, marketplace data, saved items, reports, messages, and server-side account deletion;
- Cloudflare R2 for public vehicle image delivery;
- Vercel for the Motokah web service and public marketplace pages;
- Resend for transactional account email;
- Apple Maps or the device's native mapping capability when a user opens a location;
- the device phone, messaging, share, and web capabilities when a user chooses to contact a seller.

Motokah does not use a third-party payment processor and does not sell digital content.

## 5. Regional behaviour

Core app functionality is consistent in every supported region. Marketplace inventory, default country, cities, displayed currency, and available seller contact details vary according to the selected country and the listings available there. Users can change country and location from within the app and can browse inventory in other supported countries.

## 6. Regulation and third-party material

Motokah is a classified vehicle marketplace, not a financial service, lender, insurer, transport operator, or vehicle dealer. It does not process a vehicle transaction or hold customer funds. Vehicle makes and model names are used descriptively to identify seller-submitted vehicles. Listing images and information are supplied or authorized by the relevant seller or dealer. Users can report content and block sellers from a listing page.
