# Motokah Customer Acquisition Sprint

## Target For First 14 Days
- 20 dealer conversations
- 10 dealer pages claimed
- 100 buyer WhatsApp/call clicks
- 300 Instagram followers
- 1,000 branded searches or direct visits

## Priority
Start with Tanzania. Dar es Salaam first, then Arusha, Mwanza and Zanzibar/coastal boat traffic. Kenya comes second after Tanzania pages feel active.

## Daily Operating Rhythm
- Morning: post one clean Instagram reel/feed post from existing vehicle inventory.
- Midday: message 10 dealers with their Motokah page link.
- Afternoon: ask 3 dealers for missing phone numbers or fresh stock.
- Evening: post one story with a buyer CTA: "Search before you call."

## Dealer Pitch
Your cars are already online, but buyers cannot search them properly. Motokah turns your stock into a clean dealer page buyers can find by city, make, price and body type. Send us your WhatsApp number and we will connect enquiries directly to you.

## Buyer Pitch
Before calling sellers, compare vehicles on Motokah by photos, year, mileage, price, city and dealer. Save time, avoid weak listings and contact the seller directly when a car looks right.

## First Dealer List To Chase
- Mgaya Motors TZ
- Al-Husnain Motors
- Khushi Motors
- Ibaraki Motors
- Expert Motors TZ
- Nicolette boat listings

## Content To Avoid
No accident news, fuel-price news, politics, generic RSS/news posts or thin motivational posts. Every post should point to vehicles, dealers, search, safety or direct marketplace value.

## Fastest Route To Real Customers
1. Pick 30 best vehicles with clean images and prices.
2. Turn each into one Instagram story and one WhatsApp-forwardable image.
3. Send dealer page links to dealers and ask them to share the page.
4. Ask every dealer for 5 more vehicles and a public WhatsApp number.
5. Use every enquiry as proof: "buyers are already calling from Motokah."
